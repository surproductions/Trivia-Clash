## Privacy

**App Privacy questionnaire:** *Data Not Collected*. The app has no servers, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. Display names travel only over a direct local connection to other players in the same room, and the developer never receives them.

Privacy policy text:

> Trivia Clash doesn't collect, store or share personal data on any server. Your display name, badge, settings, statistics and the list of recently played questions are stored only on your device and can be deleted in Settings › Delete all my data. When you join a nearby game, your display name and badge are sent directly to the other players' devices in that room over an encrypted local connection, to show them in the lobby and scoreboard. The app uses your local network only to find and connect to nearby games. There is no tracking and no advertising.

**Permissions used:** Local Network (`NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`) for nearby play only. There are no camera, microphone, photo, contact or location permissions.

**Encryption export compliance:** `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`. The app uses only Apple's built-in encryption (CryptoKit, Multipeer Connectivity).

## Review notes for Apple

> Trivia Clash needs no login. To test solo play: Solo › Start. To test multiplayer you need two devices on the same Wi-Fi: on device A tap Host a game › Open room. On device B tap Join a game and either tap the room shown under "Nearby rooms" or type the 5-letter code. On B tap "I'm ready", then on A tap Start match. Allow Local Network access when prompted.
